Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2025

Mylota is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Mylota mobile application (“App”). Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you sign up or log in, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile Data: You may provide information such as your name, age, gender, and fitness goals.

b. Usage Data

  • Activity Tracking: We collect data about your fitness activities, sleep schedule, water intake, mental stimulation exercises, and progress to personalize your experience.
  • Reminders & Notifications: The app may request access to your device’s notification system to send reminders for workouts, hydration, sleep, and tasks.

c. Device Information

Device ID & OS: We may collect information about your device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers for analytics and troubleshooting.

d. Contacts (Optional)

If you use features that require contact access (e.g., sharing progress), we may request permission to access your contacts. This data is used only for the intended feature and not stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized fitness, wellness, and mental stimulation recommendations.
  • To track your progress and display analytics.
  • To send reminders and notifications for scheduled activities.
  • To improve app functionality and user experience.
  • For customer support and troubleshooting.

3. How We Share Your Information

  • Service Providers: We may share data with third-party services (e.g., Firebase, Stripe) for authentication, cloud storage, and payment processing.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption and secure storage. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Choices

  • You can access and update your profile information in the app.
  • You may opt out of notifications at any time via your device settings.
  • You may delete your account and associated data by contacting support or using in-app options.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mylota does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us for removal.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app and on our website. Your continued use of the app after changes indicates your acceptance.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@mylota.com

9. User Account Deletion Policy

The personal information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this privacy policy.
